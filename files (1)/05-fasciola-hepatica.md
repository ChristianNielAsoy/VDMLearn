## Overview

Fascioliasis is a **trematode** (fluke) disease of ruminants, with spread through water and snails. Humans can be infected too.

## Key terms

trematode, operculated egg, miracidium, sporocyst, redia, cercaria, metacercaria, Lymnaeidae, definitive host, intermediate host, cholangitis, bottle jaw

## Content

**Life cycle (see diagram)**

1. Adults live in the bile ducts of the definitive host and lay eggs.
2. Eggs pass in the feces.
3. In water, the egg embryonates and a miracidium hatches.
4. The miracidium penetrates a lymnaeid snail.
5. In the snail: sporocyst, then redia (asexual multiplication).
6. Cercariae leave the snail.
7. They encyst on vegetation as **metacercariae** (the infective stage).
8. The host eats the metacercariae while grazing.
9. They excyst in the small intestine and penetrate the gut wall.
10. Juveniles migrate through the liver to the bile ducts and mature.

**Species note:** In tropical Asia, including the Philippines, _F. gigantica_ is the species usually reported in cattle and carabao (confirm with a local reference). The two species share the same overall cycle.

**Disease**

- Acute (heavy burden of migrating juveniles, esp. sheep): liver damage and hemorrhage; sudden death.
- Chronic (adults in bile ducts): cholangitis, anemia from blood feeding, low blood albumin, **submandibular edema ("bottle jaw")**, weight loss.
- Migrating flukes can set up conditions for infectious necrotic hepatitis (black disease) in sheep.

**Diagnosis:** Fecal **sedimentation** for large, operculated, yellow-brown eggs (a sedimentation test is used because the eggs are heavy). Serology and coproantigen tests detect infection earlier or confirm it. Eggs appear only after the flukes mature, so early infection can be missed.

**Control:** Break the snail link (drain or fence wet areas), manage grazing, and use strategic treatment. Choose products, timing and withdrawal periods from the label and a veterinary reference. Treat according to the stage of infection.

## High-yield

- Infective stage = metacercaria on vegetation.
- Intermediate host = lymnaeid snail.
- Eggs are operculated; use sedimentation.
- Bottle jaw means hypoalbuminemia.

## Mnemonic

**E-M-S-R-C-M** (egg, miracidium, sporocyst, redia, cercaria, metacercaria): "**E**very **M**ean **S**nail **R**eleases **C**ute **M**etacercariae."
