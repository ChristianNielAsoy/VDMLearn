## Overview

Every body system depends on membranes that control what enters and leaves cells, and on water being distributed in predictable compartments.

## Key terms

organelle, selectively permeable, diffusion, osmosis, tonicity, facilitated diffusion, active transport, Na+/K+-ATPase, endocytosis, exocytosis, intracellular fluid (ICF), extracellular fluid (ECF), interstitial fluid, plasma

## Content

**Organelles**

| Organelle       | Function                                      |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Nucleus         | DNA; controls gene expression                 |
| Mitochondrion   | ATP production (aerobic respiration)          |
| Rough ER        | Makes proteins for export or membranes        |
| Smooth ER       | Lipid synthesis, detoxification, Ca2+ storage |
| Golgi apparatus | Modifies, sorts and packages proteins         |
| Lysosome        | Intracellular digestion                       |
| Ribosome        | Protein synthesis                             |
| Peroxisome      | Breaks down fatty acids and hydrogen peroxide |

**Transport**

- Simple diffusion: down a concentration gradient, no energy (O2, CO2, lipid-soluble molecules).
- Facilitated diffusion: down the gradient through carriers or channels (e.g., glucose carriers); no ATP.
- Osmosis: water moves across a membrane toward the side with the higher solute concentration.
- Primary active transport uses ATP: the Na+/K+-ATPase moves 3 Na+ out and 2 K+ in per ATP.
- Secondary active transport uses the Na+ gradient to move another solute.
- Vesicular transport: endocytosis and exocytosis.

**Tonicity:** In an isotonic solution (e.g., 0.9% NaCl) cells keep their size; in hypotonic cells swell; in hypertonic cells shrink.

**Body fluid compartments (approximate adult values)**

- Total body water about 60% of body weight (higher in neonates).
- ICF about 40% of body weight (about 2/3 of body water); main ions K+, phosphate, proteins.
- ECF about 20% of body weight (about 1/3): interstitial about 15%, plasma about 5%; main ions Na+, Cl-, HCO3-.
- Dehydration mostly comes out of the ECF first; edema is excess interstitial fluid.

## High-yield

- Na+/K+ pump: 3 out, 2 in.
- Water follows solute (osmosis).
- The 60-40-20 rule (water, ICF, ECF as % of body weight).

## Mnemonic

**"3 out, 2 in":** the Na+/K+ pump sends 3 Na+ out and brings 2 K+ in. **"60-40-20":** body water, ICF, ECF as % of body weight.
