## Overview

Dose problems always follow the same chain: weight, dose rate, total dose, concentration, volume. Write the units on every line; they must cancel.

## Key terms

dose rate (mg/kg), concentration (mg/mL), percent w/v, ratio strength, dilution, deficit, maintenance, drip rate (gtt/min), withdrawal period

## Content

**Core formulas**

- Total dose (mg) = dose rate (mg/kg) x body weight (kg)
- Volume (mL) = total dose (mg) / concentration (mg/mL)
- Percent w/v: 1% = 1 g/100 mL = 10 mg/mL (so 2% = 20 mg/mL)
- Ratio strength: 1:1000 = 1 g/1000 mL = 1 mg/mL
- Weight: kg = lb / 2.2 (approximate)
- Dilution: C1 x V1 = C2 x V2
- Fluid deficit (L) = body weight (kg) x % dehydration (as a decimal)
- Drip rate (gtt/min) = [volume (mL/h) x drops per mL] / 60

**Worked examples (all numbers are practice values)**

1. Dog 22 lb. Drug X dose rate 5 mg/kg; stock 25 mg/mL.
   22 / 2.2 = 10 kg; 5 x 10 = 50 mg; 50 / 25 = **2 mL**.
2. Dose 150 mg; stock is a 2% solution (20 mg/mL): 150 / 20 = **7.5 mL**.
3. Make 100 mL of 5% from a 50% stock: V1 = (5 x 100) / 50 = **10 mL stock + 90 mL diluent**.
4. Fluids (practice values): 20 kg dog, 8% dehydrated, maintenance assumed 50 mL/kg/day.
   Deficit = 20 x 0.08 = 1.6 L = 1,600 mL. Maintenance = 20 x 50 = 1,000 mL/day. Total over 24 h = 2,600 mL = about 108 mL/h.
   With a 15 gtt/mL set: 108.3 x 15 / 60 = **about 27 gtt/min**.
   (Real maintenance values and replacement plans differ by species and source: always use your reference.)
5. Withdrawal period: last treatment 10 January with a 14-day withdrawal: 14 days after treatment is **24 January**. Products differ in how the days are counted (for example, whether the day of treatment counts), so follow the label's rule.

## High-yield

- Check units first; check the answer for plausibility (is 20 mL really a sensible volume?).
- Percent solution to mg/mL: multiply the percent by 10.
- Withdrawal periods protect consumers: slaughter withdrawal and milk withholding are different periods, so read the label for each.

## Mnemonic

**W-D-C-V:** Weight, Dose rate, Concentration, Volume (the order of the chain).
